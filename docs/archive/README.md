# Archive

Historical snapshots of `aiseo-audit` documentation and planning records, frozen at the point noted in each file's banner.

Files here are preserved **verbatim** (only a deprecation banner is prepended). They are intentionally not corrected or updated, even where later peer-reviewed research contradicted their claims, because they serve two purposes:

1. **Historical tracking:** an honest record of what the tool claimed at each release.
2. **Future v1-vs-v2 analysis:** comparing v1 scoring and its research rationale against the evidence-tiered 2.0 model requires the original text, not a retouched version.

For current, evidence-checked material see [../EMERGING_RESEARCH.md](../EMERGING_RESEARCH.md), [../paper-reviews/](../paper-reviews/README.md), and [../EVIDENCE.md](../EVIDENCE.md).
