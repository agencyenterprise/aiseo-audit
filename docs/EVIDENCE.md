# Factor Evidence Traceability

Last updated: 2026-08-28

Every scored or reported factor in `aiseo-audit` 2.0 maps to a row in this document. Each row states what tier of evidence backs the factor, which pipeline stage it belongs to, which paper reviews support or constrain it, the experimental regime that evidence comes from, and the metric that was actually measured. The paper reviews live in [paper-reviews/](paper-reviews/README.md); the cross-paper synthesis lives in [EMERGING_RESEARCH.md](EMERGING_RESEARCH.md).

## Evidence tiers

| Tier             | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **supported**    | Direct evidence in the factor's own stage and regime, from a strong controlled design or replicated across papers. Prerequisites by construction (a page that cannot be fetched cannot be cited) also sit here. |
| **conditional**  | Real evidence exists, but it is domain-, engine-, regime-, or starting-quality-dependent. Scored with caveats, caps, or floors rather than as a monotonic positive.                                             |
| **heuristic**    | Plausible mechanism or adjacent evidence; no direct validation of the factor as measured. Low weight, honest labeling.                                                                                          |
| **diagnostic**   | Reported but unscored. Either the isolated causal test came back null, or the evidence is purely observational.                                                                                                 |
| **experimental** | No outcome evidence at all. Reported for information only, excluded from every score.                                                                                                                           |

## Pipeline stages

| Stage  | Name                           | What it covers                                                                             |
| ------ | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **TE** | Technical eligibility          | Fetch, extraction, crawler access: can engines get usable text at all                      |
| **RA** | Retrieval alignment            | Structural fields, terms, and entities that get a page retrieved and reranked into context |
| **CF** | Citation and synthesis fitness | Content properties that make an in-context page get cited and used                         |
| **PR** | Provenance and fidelity        | Authorship, organizational identity, attribution hygiene                                   |

Diagnostics and experimental factors carry no stage: they contribute to no stage score.

## Experimental regimes

- **fixed-context:** candidate documents are already retrieved and frozen; only generation-stage effects are measured.
- **end-to-end:** full retrieval, reranking, and generation pipeline; stage-level effects are separable.
- **causal-pairwise:** matched document pairs differing in exactly one factor, randomized and repeated; supports causal claims within its domain.
- **observational:** measurement of deployed systems without any content intervention.

Two metric families recur below and must not be conflated: **word-count visibility** (how much of the answer reflects your page once it is used) and **citation rank / first-citation odds** (whether your page is cited at all, or first). Several factors help on one and are null or negative on the other; conflating them is how the original GEO numbers were over-claimed.

**On weights:** every numeric weight and threshold in the 2.0 scoring model is an expert-set heuristic, not a research-derived probability. That remains true until the calibration study described in [EMERGING_RESEARCH.md, "Requirements for Calling the Score Scientifically Validated"](EMERGING_RESEARCH.md#requirements-for-calling-the-score-scientifically-validated) is run.

## Maintenance

Adding a factor to `aiseo-audit` requires two entries, in the same change:

1. A row in this document, with tier, stage, evidence links, regime, and metric filled in (or an explicit "no evidence" statement).
2. A registry entry in the factor registry, carrying the same tier and stage so reports and scores stay consistent with this table.

A factor without a row here should be treated as undocumented and blocked from release.
